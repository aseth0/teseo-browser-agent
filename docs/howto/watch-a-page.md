# Watch a page and continue

Start something slow, go on with other work, and let the chat finish the job once the page is ready.

**Example:** you asked `reports.example.com` for a yearly sales report. It takes about twenty minutes, then shows a
**Download** link.

## What you need

- The page open in a tab.
- Notifications allowed for your browser in your system's settings, to hear about it while you are elsewhere.

## What you type

> Watch this tab until the Download link appears, then click it.

## What you see

1. A card asks first (below). Once allowed, the chat is free again: you can keep using it.
2. A watch button shows in the side panel's top bar. It lists the watch, what it waits for, the minutes left, and
   **Cancel**.
3. When the link appears, a system notification says *Watch met on reports.example.com*. Clicking it brings the tab
   to the front.
4. The conversation goes on by itself: the model clicks the link. If you are in another conversation, open the
   watch button's menu and click **Continue**.

## What you approve

- **The watch**: a card *Watch the tab "…" until "Download" appears*, every time. Click **Allow**.
- **What comes after** (the click on **Download**) asks as usual on a site set to **Ask before acting**.

## If it goes wrong

- **No notification**: check that your system allows the browser's notifications.
- **The watch ended early**: it ends when the tab is closed, goes to another site, or after 60 minutes (up to
  12 hours in **Settings → Limits**). The watch menu says why; **Dismiss** clears it. Ask for a new watch.
- **Closing the browser** ends every watch.
- **Wait for what the page shows**, not a time: *"until the Download link appears"* works better than *"in twenty
  minutes"*.
